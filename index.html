Good to know

Straight answers before you buy.

Do customers need an app?

No. Most phones made in the last few years read NFC tags by default. Hold the phone near the card and a link appears.

Can I change the link later?

Usually, yes. NFC chips can be rewritten, so get in touch and we’ll update your link.

How do I pay?

Card payments are handled by Yoco on their secure page. We never see or store your card details.

How long does delivery take?

We program and test your cards, then dispatch by courier. Delivery times are confirmed by email once your order is paid.
